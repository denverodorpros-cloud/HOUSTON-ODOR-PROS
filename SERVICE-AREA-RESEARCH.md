# Houston service-area research

Researched November 9, 2026. Proposed operating area for Houston Odor Pros; city inclusion does not establish a confirmed appointment or a business location in that city.

## Recommendation

Use Greater Houston as the core area. Accept regional requests across Southeast Texas within approximately 150 miles by road, with extended jobs toward Austin and San Antonio up to about 200 miles reviewed individually. Calculate actual travel from the eventual Houston dispatch address before confirming a job or travel fee. Avoid promising that every address inside a circular radius is serviceable.

Core communities: Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, Richmond, Pearland, Pasadena, League City, Friendswood, Baytown, Humble, Kingwood, Spring, Cypress, Tomball, The Woodlands, and Conroe.

Regional routes: Galveston and Texas City; Lake Jackson and Freeport; Beaumont, Port Arthur, and Orange; Brenham; Bryan–College Station; Huntsville, Livingston, and Lufkin. These are representative communities on Houston-area coastal, eastern, northwestern, and northern routes, not separately verified dispatch distances.

Outer requests: Austin and San Antonio, subject to exact address and road distance. Dallas/Fort Worth and Corpus Christi are not advertised as routine Houston service areas. Lake Charles is geographically within the broad distance range, but Louisiana is excluded from the Texas launch until interstate operation is explicitly confirmed.

## Sources

- [Visit Houston — Around Houston destinations](https://www.visithoustontexas.com/aroundhouston/destinations/): regional context for Bay Area Houston, Baytown, Beaumont, Brazosport, Galveston, Huntsville, and Texas City.
- [Visit Brenham — Plan your visit](https://visitbrenhamtexas.com/plan-your-visit/): describes Brenham as approximately 75 miles northwest of Houston.
- [Beaumont Convention & Visitors Bureau — Maps](https://www.beaumontcvb.com/plan/maps/): describes Beaumont as 90 miles east of Houston.
- [Visit Austin — Getting here](https://www.austintexas.org/plan-a-trip/getting-here/): lists Houston–Austin at 165 miles. Treat as a regional planning estimate, not an address-specific route measurement.
- [TxDOT — Texas State Travel Guide media brochure](https://plans.txdot.gov/pub/txdot-info/trv/travel-industry/travel-guide-media.pdf): lists Houston–San Antonio at 197 miles. Older publication; use only as a broad geographic estimate and reconfirm actual routes before dispatch.
- [TxDOT — Traveling Across Texas mileage grid](https://www.txdot.gov/content/dam/docs/division/trv/city-maps/travel-map-mileage-between-cities-grid.pdf): official resource for estimating intercity distances; does not establish an operating radius or appointment availability.
- [Visit Lake Charles — Location](https://www.visitlakecharles.org/get-to-know-lake-charles/): approximately 140 miles from Houston, explaining why the broad geographic radius crosses a state boundary. Not included in advertised launch coverage.
