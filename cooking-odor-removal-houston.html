Cooking and food odor treatment

Cooking residues can collect on cabinets, walls, nearby fabrics, and other surfaces. Our odor treatment focuses on lingering smells after spoiled food, spills, grease deposits, and other odor sources have been cleaned or removed.

Common projects

  • Lingering frying, grease, and strong spice smells
  • Burnt-food odors after the source has been removed
  • Apartment and rental turnover after frequent cooking
  • Homes being prepared for sale, showings, or move-in

Preparation comes first

Before treatment, remove spoiled food and trash, clean affected surfaces, and address grease residue. Range-hood filters, upholstery, or other affected materials may need separate cleaning or replacement. We explain preparation and the recommended scope before the visit.

Unoccupied treatment and ventilation

If ozone treatment is appropriate, people, pets, and plants must be out of the treated area. We explain ventilation and re-entry instructions before normal use resumes. Odor treatment does not replace grease cleaning, appliance repair, or ventilation repairs.

Share your city, odor source, affected rooms, and preferred timing. Our team will review the details and provide a personalized estimate.
